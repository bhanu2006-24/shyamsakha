# Audio Assets Directory

You can place your custom audio files here. The in-game `SoundManager` includes procedural Web Audio synthesizers (flute ragas, ball bounces, splashes, bells) that work out of the box, and also seamlessly supports custom audio files!

### Recommended Audio Files:
- `flute_bgm.mp3` or `flute_melody.mp3` - Background flute/bhajan music.
- `ball_hit.wav` or `ball_hit.mp3` - Sound when bumping or hitting the volleyball.
- `ball_spike.wav` - Heavy spike sound in Kanduka Kridha.
- `ball_bounce.wav` - Ball bouncing on the sand or ground.
- `splash.wav` - Yamuna water splashing or Holi pichkari squirt.
- `cow_bell.wav` - Tinkling bells of Gau Mata.
- `cheer.wav` - Cheers from Sakhas when scoring a point.

You can configure and enable them in `js/core/SoundManager.js`.
